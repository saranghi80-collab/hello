    How it works

    Cruise drive speed scales with your distance to the nearest surface, so point at a world and the ship slows itself on approach. Drop to flight within a few thousand kilometres, lower the gear and descend slowly to land. Skim close to a star, or through a gas giant's upper atmosphere, to refuel; watch your heat. Jumps cross light-years at nearly the speed of light: a few weeks pass aboard, and as many years at home as the light-years you travel.

    Off the books

    The boost raises the cruise drive's ceiling from 2,400 c to as much as 240 million c, and lets flight mode build speed much faster. The drive still stops you short of a surface. Without invincibility, air at those speeds will burn you up. Nothing gets you out of a black hole.
